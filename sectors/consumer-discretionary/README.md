# Consumer Discretionary

[Company index](INDEX.md) · [Master index](../../INDEX.md)

## Sector overview

Consumer businesses whose demand is sensitive to income, confidence, and discretionary spending.

This page is a research guide. It does not contain a completed sector investment assessment.

## Industry map

The map below uses examples from the SEC industry-code list to organize research. It is a project grouping, not an official GICS constituent classification. A reported SIC code describes a business category; it does not establish every activity of a diversified issuer.

| Business family | Reported SIC examples | What to establish from company evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home construction and household goods | 1520, 1531, 2510, 3630 | Check housing demand, financing sensitivity, orders, cancellations and inventories. |
| Vehicles and related products | 3711, 3714, 3716, 3751 | Separate manufacturers, component suppliers and dealers; reconcile units, price and product mix. |
| Retail and direct commerce | 5311, 5399, 5600, 5700, 5961 | Identify inventory ownership, physical versus digital distribution, returns and comparable sales definitions. |
| Restaurants, lodging and recreation | 5812, 7011, 7948, 7990 | Separate owned operations from franchise or management fees; distinguish capacity from utilization. |

## Research questions

- How sensitive is demand to employment, credit, and consumer confidence?
- What drives brand strength, inventory risk, and distribution economics?

## Metrics to investigate

- Comparable sales
- Units and average selling price
- Inventory turnover
- Customer acquisition and retention

Select metrics that fit each business model. These are research prompts, not reported figures or standardized definitions across all companies.
