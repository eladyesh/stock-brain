# Health Care

[Company index](INDEX.md) · [Master index](../../INDEX.md)

## Sector overview

Health care providers, equipment, pharmaceuticals, biotechnology, and related services.

This page is a research guide. It does not contain a completed sector investment assessment.

## Industry map

The map below uses examples from the SEC industry-code list to organize research. It is a project grouping, not an official GICS constituent classification. A reported SIC code describes a business category; it does not establish every activity of a diversified issuer.

| Business family | Reported SIC examples | What to establish from company evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Medicines and biological products | 2833, 2834, 2835, 2836 | Separate approved products from development programs; track product-level sales, patent exposure and dated regulatory evidence. |
| Medical devices and equipment | 3841, 3842, 3843, 3844, 3845, 3851 | Distinguish equipment installations, procedure-linked consumables and service contracts. |
| Care providers and diagnostics | 8011, 8050, 8051, 8060, 8062, 8071, 8082, 8090, 8093 | Investigate reimbursement, labor costs, patient or test volumes and payer concentration. |
| Health-product distribution | 5047, 5122 | Separate distribution margins, working capital and contract concentration from manufacturer economics. |

## Research questions

- What clinical, regulatory, reimbursement, and patent events change the outlook?
- How dependent is the business on individual products or programs?

## Metrics to investigate

- Product or service revenue
- Research and development spending
- Patient or procedure volumes
- Pipeline milestones and patent timelines

Select metrics that fit each business model. These are research prompts, not reported figures or standardized definitions across all companies.
