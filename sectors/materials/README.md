## Sector overview

Chemicals, metals, mining, construction materials, packaging, and forest products.

This page is a research guide. It does not contain a completed sector investment assessment.

## Industry map

The map below uses examples from the SEC industry-code list to organize research. It is a project grouping, not an official GICS constituent classification. A reported SIC code describes a business category; it does not establish every activity of a diversified issuer.

| Business family | Reported SIC examples | What to establish from company evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mining and metals | 1000, 1040, 1090, 3310, 3312, 3330, 3334 | Identify the resource and processing stage; record grades, recovery, shipment volumes, costs and capital requirements. |
| Chemicals and plastics | 2800, 2810, 2820, 2821, 2860, 2870, 2890, 3080 | Separate commodity chemicals from specialized formulations; investigate feedstocks, capacity and customer applications. |
| Forest products, paper and packaging | 0800, 2400, 2421, 2600, 2611, 2621, 2631, 2650, 2670 | Distinguish resource ownership, mills and converting; reconcile volumes, pricing and fiber or energy costs. |
| Construction materials | 1400, 3211, 3241, 3250, 3270, 3272, 3281 | Check local supply, shipping distance, construction demand and plant or quarry capacity. |

The [company index](INDEX.md) lists actual factual files, their classification basis and financial-data availability. Ambiguous and fund-related records remain in the [unclassified directory](../unclassified/INDEX.md). Individual products, customers, competitive positions and investment conclusions still require company-specific sources.

## Research questions

- How do supply, demand, commodity prices, and input costs interact?
- What resource, process, or logistical advantages support economics?

## Metrics to investigate

- Production and shipment volumes
- Realized prices and spreads
- Unit costs
- Capacity and capital expenditure

Select metrics that fit each business model. These are research prompts, not reported figures or standardized definitions across all companies.
