# Real Estate

[Company index](INDEX.md) · [Master index](../../INDEX.md)

## Sector overview

Property ownership, equity REITs, development, and real estate services.

This page is a research guide. It does not contain a completed sector investment assessment.

## Industry map

The map below uses examples from the SEC industry-code list to organize research. It is a project grouping, not an official GICS constituent classification. A reported SIC code describes a business category; it does not establish every activity of a diversified issuer.

| Business family | Reported SIC examples | What to establish from company evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Property ownership and leasing | 6510, 6512, 6513, 6519 | Identify property type, tenant concentration, occupancy, lease duration and debt maturities. |
| Property services and transactions | 6531, 6532 | Separate brokerage and service fees from property inventory and balance-sheet risk. |
| Land development | 6552 | Investigate land ownership, entitlements, project stages, presales and financing obligations. |
| REITs requiring classification review | 6798 | This reported code alone does not separate equity REITs from mortgage REITs. The automatic directory leaves this code unclassified until company evidence resolves it. |

The [company index](INDEX.md) lists actual factual files, their classification basis and financial-data availability. Ambiguous and fund-related records remain in the [unclassified directory](../unclassified/INDEX.md). Individual products, customers, competitive positions and investment conclusions still require company-specific sources.

## Research questions

- Which property types, tenant exposures, and lease structures drive cash flow?
- How do funding costs, occupancy, and supply affect asset values?

## Metrics to investigate

- Occupancy and leasing spreads
- Net operating income
- Funds from operations where relevant
- Debt maturities and loan-to-value

Select metrics that fit each business model. These are research prompts, not reported figures or standardized definitions across all companies.
