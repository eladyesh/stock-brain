# Information Technology

## Sector overview

Reviewed through 2026-10-18. This is a narrow pilot lens on enterprise software/cloud and memory/storage, based on Microsoft and Micron. It does not establish coverage of the whole technology sector, aggregate market size, or investable rankings. Microsoft monetizes software, services and infrastructure; Micron supplies memory and storage products. [S01] [S02]

## Industry map

Microsoft connects applications, distribution and cloud infrastructure. Micron supplies DRAM, NAND and NOR products into several end markets. These occupy different value-chain positions; their common data-center exposure does not by itself prove a supplier contract between them. [S01] [S02]

Microsoft changed its reporting structure to Agents and Infra and Devices and Consumer for FY2027. Micron reports Cloud Memory, Core Data Center, Mobile and Client, and Automotive and Embedded units. Compare economic activities before comparing issuer-specific segment labels. [S02] [S03]

## Research questions

Research interpretation: test whether infrastructure deployment translates into profitable usage, and separate recurring subscriptions from consumption and product sales. For memory, examine contract terms, qualification, technology transitions and capital intensity before extrapolating a strong quarter. These are research questions inferred from the two business models, not forecasts. [S01] [S02]

## Metrics to investigate

For Microsoft, align revenue, operating income and cash investment to the same fiscal period; separate cash property/equipment purchases from noncash lease additions. Use the FY2027 segment recast when comparing segments. For Micron, track fiscal-period revenue, gross margin, cash, debt, capacity milestones and customer deposits; a deposit is not automatically recognized revenue. No sector-wide numeric benchmark is established here. [S01] [S02] [S03]

## Cross-sector themes

Research interpretation: tags such as ai, cloud, data-centers and semiconductors connect demand and investment questions across the pilot. Shared tags identify research topics; a direct customer/supplier or competitive claim needs its own dated evidence. Product qualifications and infrastructure utilization are different checkpoints and should not be collapsed into one demand indicator. [S01] [S02]
