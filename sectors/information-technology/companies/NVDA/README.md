# NVDA — NVIDIA Corporation

[Sector](../../README.md) · [Master index](../../../../INDEX.md)

> Evidence snapshot through 2026-10-18. Research status is recorded in metadata; this is not a live market feed.

## Business overview

Reported: NVIDIA supplies accelerated-computing platforms combining processors, networking, systems and software for AI, graphics and other compute-intensive workloads. [S01]


## Revenue model and segments

Reported: Reportable segments are Compute & Networking and Graphics. The Q2 FY2027 market presentation groups revenue into Data Center and Edge Computing; those market categories are not the reportable segments. [S02]


## Products and services

Reported: CUDA and acceleration libraries accompany GPU platforms; NVIDIA also sells networking and enterprise software. The August release reports Vera Rubin ramping into full production. [S03] [S01]


## Customers and end markets

Reported: Direct buyers include distributors, OEMs, cloud providers and model makers. Revenue concentration among direct customers does not identify the ultimate users of their systems. [S02]


## Industry and value chain

Reported: NVIDIA uses outsourced manufacturing. Its annual report identifies TSMC and Samsung as foundries and Micron, SK hynix and Samsung as memory suppliers. [S01]


## Competitive landscape

Reported: AMD and Intel compete in accelerated computing; large cloud customers also design substitutes internally. Broadcom appears among networking competitors. Competition differs by product rather than mapping neatly to one sector list. [S01]


## Moat and differentiation

Interpretation: software compatibility and system integration can reinforce adoption of successive NVIDIA platforms. They do not eliminate customer incentives to develop custom accelerators or negotiate price. [S01]


## Growth drivers

Management guidance: Management links growth to AI training, inference and emerging agent workloads. Its August outlook assumes no China Data Center compute revenue, emphasizing that geographic access is a constraint. [S03]


## Financial performance

Reported: For Q2 FY2027 ended July 26, 2026, GAAP revenue was USD 96,221 million, operating income USD 63,734 million and net income USD 59,688 million. [S02]

Reported: Q2 FY2027 GAAP operating cash flow was USD 24,077 million for the quarter ended July 26, 2026, versus USD 50,344 million in Q1 ended April 26, 2026. The CFO attributed the sequential decline to higher working-capital adjustments and cash taxes. These are quarterly cash flows, not the six-month cumulative figure. [S05] [S02]

Reported: Q2 FY2027 GAAP income includes USD 7,771 million of net equity-securities gains. NVIDIA reports non-GAAP net income of USD 53,954 million after excluding these gains and applying other adjustments and related taxes. Beginning in Q1 FY2027, its non-GAAP measures include stock compensation; the comparative non-GAAP periods presented were recast on that basis. [S05] [S02]


## Balance sheet and capital allocation

Reported: At July 26, 2026, GAAP cash and equivalents were USD 22,443 million, separate from marketable investments. The subsequently announced Hugging Face acquisition remains conditional and should not be treated as completed. [S02] [S04]

Reported: At July 26, 2026, net accounts receivable were USD 63,059 million. The CFO reported days sales outstanding of 60 days, up from 45 days in the preceding quarter, due to extended payment terms on large multi-quarter agreements with certain investment-grade customers. Longer collection terms alone do not establish a credit loss. [S02] [S05]

Reported: Supply and capacity commitments were USD 279 billion at July 26, 2026, versus USD 119 billion in the previous quarter, primarily for memory and manufacturing capacity. Some supplier arrangements may be cancelled, rescheduled or adjusted before firm orders, potentially at additional cost. The commitments are future procurement obligations, not current debt or customer revenue backlog. [S02] [S05]

Reported: The August 2026 SB Energy guarantees cap NVIDIA credit support at USD 105 billion for an OpenAI affiliate. Including existing AI-cloud guarantees of USD 3.5 billion, disclosed maximum gross exposure is USD 108.5 billion. Payment depends on specified tenant defaults; guarantees activate as the relevant leases commence and their exposure declines with lease payments. Maximum exposure is not current debt, an expected loss or recognized revenue. [S02] [S05]


## Management and governance

Reported: The August 26 results identify Jensen Huang as founder and CEO and Colette Kress as executive vice president and CFO. Ownership and compensation have not been reviewed. [S03]


## Valuation context

Not available: No dated price, current capitalization, investment-adjusted enterprise value or consensus forecast is included. A valuation requires separating operating earnings from investment gains and testing AI-spending durability.

## Catalysts

Management guidance: NVIDIA expects the Hugging Face transaction to close in the first half of 2027, subject to customary conditions and regulatory approvals. Approval and integration remain uncertain. [S04]

Management guidance: As disclosed on August 26, 2026, management expects the first SB Energy phase in FY2029, subject to construction and ready-for-service conditions. The CFO commentary describes potential revenue from future infrastructure generations; those opportunities are forward-looking and are not realized revenue or a contracted sales schedule. [S05] [S02]


## Risks and thesis breakers

Interpretation: use the Q2 FY2027 receivables, collection days and quarterly operating cash flow as monitoring baselines. Collection days rising further while cash generation fails to recover, unused supply commitments increasing, delayed customer deployments or a tenant default that calls a guarantee would weaken the growth and funding thesis. Extended terms for investment-grade customers are not by themselves evidence of impairment; track actual collections and guarantee utilization. [S02] [S05]


## Scenarios and open questions

Interpretation: bull requires sustained deployment of new platforms; base assumes orderly transitions; bear combines customer delays and competing accelerators. Investigate deployment economics and repeat demand rather than treating announced capacity as realized revenue. [S03]


## Relationships and themes

Reported: Hugging Face is a proposed acquisition target, not yet a subsidiary in this packet. NVIDIA commits to an open platform supporting other silicon vendors if the transaction completes. [S04]


## Recent developments

Reported: NVIDIA signed the Hugging Face agreement on September 2 and disclosed it September 3, 2026. This extends the research focus from hardware deployment to model-development tooling and acquisition execution. [S04]
