# PLTR — Palantir Technologies Inc.

[Sector](../../README.md) · [Master index](../../../../INDEX.md)

> Evidence snapshot through 2026-10-18. Research status is recorded in metadata; this is not a live market feed.

## Business overview

Reported: Palantir sells software that connects organizational data to operational decisions for commercial and government users. PLTR common stock is listed on Nasdaq in the 2026 quarterly filing. [S02]


## Revenue model and segments

Reported: Government and commercial are separate operating segments. Revenue is generally recognized across contract terms, so current revenue can lag changes in new sales or renewals. [S01]


## Products and services

Reported: Foundry integrates data and workflows through the Ontology; AIP connects AI models to governed operations; Apollo delivers software updates; Gotham supports defense and intelligence missions. [S01]


## Customers and end markets

Reported: Customers span government and commercial organizations. Government procurement and commercial deployment differ; multi-period contracts and termination provisions make contracted deal value an imperfect proxy for future recognized revenue. [S01]


## Industry and value chain

Interpretation: AIP connects third-party, open-source, or self-hosted models with customer data and actions. Interpretation: Palantir sells operational integration and governance rather than being interchangeable with the underlying model suppliers. [S01]


## Competitive landscape

Reported: Palantir identifies internal software development as a central alternative, alongside enterprise software vendors, government contractors, and systems integrators. The issuer does not provide a complete ranked competitor map. [S01]


## Moat and differentiation

Interpretation: an Ontology embedded in operational workflows can create switching costs. The claim depends on durable customer use and value; product descriptions alone cannot establish retention or pricing power. [S01]


## Growth drivers

Management guidance: On August 3 management raised full-year 2026 revenue guidance to USD 8,150–8,158 million. This forecast depends on continued deployment and expansion; it is neither earned revenue nor consensus. [S03]


## Financial performance

Reported: For Q2 2026 ended June 30, GAAP revenue was USD 1,935.464 million, operating income USD 912.004 million, and net income attributable to common stockholders USD 1,061.890 million. Diluted GAAP EPS was USD 0.41. Common-stockholder net income differs from consolidated net income. [S02]


## Balance sheet and capital allocation

Interpretation: At June 30, cash and equivalents were USD 2,030.047 million and marketable securities USD 7,379.052 million. Q2 GAAP stock-based compensation expense was USD 265.209 million. Interpretation: assess equity compensation and dilution alongside cash generation. [S02]


## Management and governance

Reported: The August release identifies Alex Karp as CEO. The quarterly filing describes founder-controlled Class F voting rights subject to an ownership threshold; ordinary shareholders should distinguish economic ownership from voting influence. [S03] [S02]


## Valuation context

Not available: A current market price and forward consensus were not sourced. Any valuation must distinguish GAAP earnings, adjusted measures, and dilution; no current multiple or target is supplied.

## Catalysts

Management guidance: The August 3 outlook called for Q3 2026 revenue of USD 2,160–2,164 million. The next results will test that guidance; an independently verified release date was not retained in this packet. [S03]


## Risks and thesis breakers

Interpretation: long sales cycles, contract termination, deployment friction, and data or AI governance failures can impede growth. Repeated weak conversion of deals to durable production revenue would challenge the adoption thesis. [S03]


## Scenarios and open questions

Interpretation: Bull: customers expand production use; base: deployment remains uneven; bear: internal alternatives or procurement constraints slow adoption. These are assumptions. Contract durability, customer-level economics, and the recurring role of investment gains remain open questions. [S02]


## Relationships and themes

Reported: The 2024 Microsoft partnership pairs Palantir platforms with Azure government and classified environments. Classified availability was subject to authorization. This establishes an integration relationship, not exclusivity or proof that all announced deployments occurred. [S04]


## Recent developments

Reported: August 3, 2026: Palantir reported Q2 results and raised full-year guidance. The August 4 quarterly filing supplies GAAP statements and updated risk disclosures; adjusted operating income and cash-flow measures require their separate reconciliations. [S03] [S02]
