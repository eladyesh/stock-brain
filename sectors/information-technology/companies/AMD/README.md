# AMD — Advanced Micro Devices, Inc.

[Sector](../../README.md) · [Master index](../../../../INDEX.md)

> Evidence snapshot through 2026-10-18. Research status is recorded in metadata; this is not a live market feed.

## Business overview

Reported: AMD designs processors, accelerators and adaptive-computing products sold into data centers, PCs, gaming and embedded systems. Manufacturing relies on external foundries. [S01]


## Revenue model and segments

Reported: The Q2 2026 report uses Data Center, Client and Gaming, and Embedded segments. Client and Gaming are combined for segment reporting, despite separate business revenue disclosures. [S03]


## Products and services

Reported: Core families include EPYC server CPUs, Instinct accelerators, Ryzen PC processors, Radeon graphics and adaptive SoCs/FPGAs. Software tools support deployment and programmability. [S01]


## Customers and end markets

Reported: AMD sells through OEMs, hyperscalers, distributors and system integrators. Certain customers pay engineering fees for design and development and purchase the resulting chips. [S01]


## Industry and value chain

Reported: TSMC supplies wafers for high-performance and adaptive products; GlobalFoundries supplies specified older process nodes. Product execution therefore depends on both design and outsourced capacity. [S01]


## Competitive landscape

Reported: Intel competes in CPUs; NVIDIA competes in GPUs and data-center acceleration. Customer-designed processors and accelerators also constrain the addressable market. [S01]


## Moat and differentiation

Interpretation: a broad compute portfolio and software support can aid system adoption, but competitiveness requires timely launches, power efficiency and customer qualification. Portfolio breadth alone does not prove pricing power. [S01]


## Growth drivers

Management guidance: Management expects EPYC demand, Instinct deployments and the Helios ramp to support second-half 2026 growth. These are management expectations, not a committed revenue schedule. [S04]


## Financial performance

Reported: For Q2 2026 ended June 27, GAAP revenue was USD 11,536 million, operating income USD 1,990 million and net income USD 2,297 million. Prior-year comparisons require considering the MI308 export-control inventory charge. [S03]

Management guidance: On August 4, 2026, AMD guided Q3 2026 revenue to approximately USD 13 billion, plus or minus USD 300 million. It guided non-GAAP gross margin to approximately 56%, operating expenses to approximately USD 3.65 billion, other income net of interest expense to approximately USD 55 million, and an effective tax rate of 13%. Except revenue, these outlook measures are non-GAAP; they are dated management expectations, not reported Q3 results. [S08]

Reported: Q2 2026 GAAP earnings include USD 483 million of net gains on long-term investments. The earnings reconciliation removes these gains when presenting non-GAAP income and separately adjusts stock compensation, acquired-intangible amortization, other costs and taxes. GAAP net income and the adjusted outlook therefore use different accounting bases. [S08] [S03]


## Balance sheet and capital allocation

Reported: AMD completed a senior-note offering on August 17, 2026, after the quarter-end balance sheet. In September it agreed to acquire World Labs using shares, so quarter-end debt and share counts are insufficient for a current capitalization. [S05] [S07]

Management guidance: The August 4, 2026 presentation estimates Q3 2026 diluted weighted-average shares at 1,661 million for GAAP and non-GAAP EPS. The employee-equity dilution estimate uses an average stock price of USD 534 from June 29 through July 24, 2026. That historical assumption is not a current stock quote, and the estimate predates the World Labs acquisition agreement. [S08] [S07]

Reported: At June 27, 2026, AMD reported approximately USD 30.3 billion of unconditional commitments, including USD 17.4 billion for the remainder of fiscal 2026, and data-center lease guarantees with maximum potential future payments of USD 4.1 billion. Purchase commitments and contingent guarantees are funding exposures, not customer revenue backlog. [S03]


## Management and governance

Reported: The earnings release names Lisa Su as chair/CEO and Jean Hu as CFO. The August 19 board change appointed Tim Ryan and made KC McClure chair of Audit and Finance. [S04] [S06]


## Valuation context

Not available: No market-price snapshot or forecast valuation is included. Update debt and potential acquisition dilution before calculating enterprise value or per-share scenarios; compare GAAP and adjusted earnings consistently.

## Catalysts

Interpretation: compare the next reported Q3 2026 revenue, non-GAAP gross margin and operating expenses with the dated August outlook, and reconcile differences to Helios deployment, Instinct shipments and Data Center growth. A missed outlook alongside delayed deployments warrants a thesis review. Product announcements alone do not establish recognized revenue. [S04] [S08]


## Risks and thesis breakers

Interpretation: the growth thesis weakens if export restrictions, memory shortages or qualification delays prevent the expected accelerator ramp while unconditional purchase commitments remain. Monitor the reported commitment schedule, cash generation and acquisition dilution alongside the dated revenue and margin outlook; persistent execution misses or funding needs unsupported by operating cash flow require reassessment. [S03] [S08] [S07]


## Scenarios and open questions

Interpretation: bull assumes successful accelerator scaling; base assumes product ramps with mixed end markets; bear assumes export restrictions and competitive displacement. Validate software readiness and customer economics before quantifying these scenarios. [S04]


## Relationships and themes

Reported: World Labs is a proposed acquisition counterparty. The September filing describes share consideration with the share count determined near closing; it does not establish that the acquisition has closed. [S07]


## Recent developments

Reported: The World Labs agreement was signed September 26 and disclosed September 28, 2026. Separately, the February 4 annual-report amendment corrected transposed Client ASP and unit-growth disclosures; use the amended MD&A for historical comparisons. [S07] [S02]
