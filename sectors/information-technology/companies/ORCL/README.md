# ORCL — Oracle Corporation

[Sector](../../README.md) · [Master index](../../../../INDEX.md)

> Evidence snapshot through 2026-10-18. Research status is recorded in metadata; this is not a live market feed.

## Business overview

Reported: Oracle supplies enterprise databases, cloud infrastructure, business applications, hardware, and services. Its NYSE common-stock ticker is ORCL. [S01]


## Revenue model and segments

Reported: FY2026 reportable businesses are cloud and software, hardware, and services. Subscription and consumption cloud revenue coexist with software licenses and support; these streams have different revenue-recognition patterns. [S01]


## Products and services

Reported: OCI supplies infrastructure; Oracle Database supports enterprise data; Fusion serves ERP, HCM, and customer workflows; NetSuite targets smaller organizations; Oracle Health serves healthcare. [S01]


## Customers and end markets

Reported: Oracle serves enterprises and government across industries. Cloud-infrastructure demand and application renewals should be assessed separately; this packet does not quantify customer concentration. [S01]


## Industry and value chain

Interpretation: cloud expansion couples software demand to datacenter construction, power, and component supply. Capacity delivery and financing are therefore operating constraints, not merely accounting details. [S03]


## Competitive landscape

Reported: Oracle names Microsoft, Amazon, Alphabet, Salesforce, SAP, and other vendors among competitors across its offerings. This broad list does not mean every named company competes in every product. [S01]


## Moat and differentiation

Interpretation: embedded databases and integrated applications may make migration costly. That hypothesis requires renewal and customer evidence; the annual report also describes intense competition and customer demand for lower complexity. [S01]


## Growth drivers

Management guidance: Management guided FY2027 revenue to at least USD 90,000 million and non-GAAP EPS to USD 8.10 on September 10. These are forecasts dependent on execution and demand, not reported results. [S03]


## Financial performance

Reported: FY2027 Q1 ended August 31, 2026: GAAP revenue USD 19,345 million, operating income USD 6,728 million, net income available to common shareholders USD 4,679 million, and diluted EPS USD 1.56. [S02]


## Balance sheet and capital allocation

Interpretation: Q1 operating cash flow of USD 23,103 million included USD 11,363 million of customer prepayments with a significant financing component. Cash capex was USD 28,499 million; net ATM equity proceeds were USD 19,909 million. Interpretation: cash generation must be assessed alongside prepayment financing and dilution. [S02]


## Management and governance

Reported: The September 25 proxy lists Clay Magouyrk and Mike Sicilia as CEOs, Hilary Maxson as CFO, and Safra Catz as executive vice chair. This supersedes older descriptions naming Catz as CEO. [S04]


## Valuation context

Not available: No current market price or forward consensus was sourced. Valuation needs diluted common equity, preferred claims, debt, leases, and funding commitments reconciled before any enterprise-value multiple.

## Catalysts

Not available: A verified date for the next earnings release was not obtained. Monitor subsequent filings for capacity conversion, customer prepayments, cash capex, and funding changes.

## Risks and thesis breakers

Interpretation: delayed capacity, weak conversion of commitments to revenue, or continued cash demands beyond accessible funding would challenge the cloud thesis. Large prepayments do not remove execution obligations. [S02] [S03]


## Scenarios and open questions

Interpretation: Bull: delivered capacity converts demand profitably. Base: expansion consumes capital before returns mature. Bear: demand or utilization disappoints while commitments persist. These are assumptions; customer concentration and contract financing require deeper review. [S03]


## Relationships and themes

Reported: The September earnings release explicitly positions Oracle AI Data Platform against Palantir-style enterprise ontologies. This is Oracle marketing positioning, not independent proof of superior capability or a commercial partnership. [S03]


## Recent developments

Reported: September 12, 2026: Oracle announced Larry Ellison cancelled his stock-sale plan, with no sales under that plan before cancellation. The September 14 filing documents the event; cancellation is not an insider purchase. [S05]
