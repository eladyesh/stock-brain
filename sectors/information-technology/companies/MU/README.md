# MU — Micron Technology, Inc.

[Sector](../../README.md) · [Master index](../../../../INDEX.md)

> Evidence snapshot through 2026-10-18. Research status is recorded in metadata; this is not a live market feed.

## Business overview

Reported: Micron designs and manufactures semiconductor memory and storage: DRAM, NAND and NOR. It sells components and integrated solutions for data centers, computers, mobile devices, vehicles and industrial systems. [S01]


## Revenue model and segments

Reported: The FY2026 reporting units are Cloud Memory, Core Data Center, Mobile and Client, and Automotive and Embedded. Cloud Memory includes hyperscaler memory and all data-center HBM; Core Data Center includes data-center storage. [S01]


## Products and services

Reported: Micron announced HBM4 volume shipments and high-volume production of SOCAMM2 low-power memory modules and its 9650 PCIe Gen6 SSD. These address different bottlenecks: accelerator memory bandwidth, CPU memory capacity and access to stored data. Performance comparisons in the announcement are issuer tests, not independent benchmarks. [S04]


## Customers and end markets

Reported: Customer contracts include short-duration purchases and multi-year take-or-pay commitments. Some strategic agreements have price floors and ceilings; payments normally follow delivery. [S01]


## Industry and value chain

Management guidance: Management says additional cleanroom space is needed to address memory supply constraints, while new fabs require gradual production ramps. Its expectation of tight supply through calendar 2028 is an outlook, not proof that memory cyclicality has ended. [S03]


## Competitive landscape

Reported: The FY2026 filing names Samsung, SK hynix, Kioxia, Sandisk, CXMT and YMTC as memory/storage competitors. Pricing and process execution remain competitive variables. [S01]


## Moat and differentiation

Interpretation: qualification within a jointly engineered accelerator platform and integration across memory and storage can make substitution harder during a platform cycle. The March product announcement supports technical integration; it does not establish durable monopoly power, independent performance leadership or guaranteed future margins. [S04]


## Growth drivers

Management guidance: Management links longer AI context windows, larger models and concurrent workloads to more memory and storage content. It also expects SSD adoption for KV-cache offload. The mechanism depends on customers deploying the systems and sustaining memory requirements. [S03]


## Financial performance

Reported: For fiscal Q4 ended September 3, 2026, GAAP revenue was USD 54,229 million, net income USD 37,701 million and gross margin 86.8%. FY2026 GAAP revenue was USD 133,188 million and net income USD 84,969 million. The fiscal year contained 53 weeks. [S02] [S01]


## Balance sheet and capital allocation

Reported: At September 3, 2026, GAAP cash and equivalents were USD 38,364 million; current debt was USD 491 million and long-term debt USD 4,688 million. These cash figures exclude investments. Customer deposits are liabilities, not earned revenue. [S02] [S01]


## Management and governance

Reported: The October 9 filing identifies Sanjay Mehrotra as chairman and CEO, Mark Murphy as CFO, and Manish Bhatia as president and COO. Executive incentives and beneficial ownership require separate proxy review. [S01]


## Valuation context

Not available: No timestamped market price, current diluted capitalization or normalized-cycle forecast has been sourced. A spot P/E would be especially sensitive to whether unusually strong memory pricing persists. No fair value or recommendation is assigned.

## Catalysts

Management guidance: Management targets initial Singapore HBM packaging output in early calendar 2027 and Idaho ID1 wafer output in mid-2027. Monitor commissioning, qualification and subsequent shipment ramp separately; initial output is not full capacity. These targets may change. [S03]


## Risks and thesis breakers

Interpretation: Selling-price declines, supply expansion and trade restrictions can hurt results. Interpretation: sustained pricing weakness or failed qualification would undermine the assumption that product differentiation protects earnings. [S01]


## Scenarios and open questions

Interpretation: Interpretive scenarios: bull assumes stronger content growth and successful product qualification; base assumes gradual capacity ramps; bear assumes supply catches demand while customers resist pricing. Track shipment mix, realized pricing and customer acceptance. Scenario probabilities and earnings estimates remain unmodeled. [S03]


## Relationships and themes

Reported: NVIDIA is a documented platform collaborator: Micron designed the announced HBM4 and SOCAMM2 offerings for Vera Rubin and optimized the 9650 SSD for BlueField-4 STX. This is a supply-chain/platform relationship, not evidence that Micron competes with NVIDIA in GPUs. [S04]


## Recent developments

Reported: On September 30, Micron reported new server-RDIMM sampling, customer qualifications and expanded SSD applications alongside fiscal-year results. [S02]

Interpretation: Sampling and qualification are product milestones, not proof of recognized sales. Future refreshes should connect these milestones to reported shipments, revenue and customer acceptance. [S02]
