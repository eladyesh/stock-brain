## Conflicts, qualifications and omissions

- No inconsistent FY2026 revenue, net-income, cash or debt amount was found between the release and annual filing at the checked locations.
- A general IR landing-page title still included president in Mehrotra's title when accessed. The packet uses the dated October 9 executive roster and September 30 prepared remarks instead; Bhatia's president/COO appointment is reflected in the annual filing.
- The March product release is historical evidence of the stated platform relationship, not proof of current shipment volumes or performance against every competitor.
- Company claims of technology leadership and supply scarcity are not independent market-share evidence. No third-party rank or quantitative competitive lead has been inferred.
- A valuation is intentionally unavailable: current price, diluted capitalization and normalized cycle assumptions have not been sourced. Beneficial ownership and incentive design require a proxy pass. More detail is needed on customer concentration, geography and contract enforceability.
- The source-backed profile is suitable for a bounded reference-profile review, with these gaps visible. It is not a complete investment diligence, a valuation or a recommendation.
