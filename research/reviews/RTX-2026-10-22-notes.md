The profit-quality caveat is material: Q3 adjusted EPS of USD 2.26 includes USD 0.25 of IEEPA recovery. Pratt's reported segment operating profit fell to USD 608m from USD 751m in the prior-year quarter while adjusted profit reached USD 968m. The adjusted measure removes the USD 363m bankruptcy charge but retains approximately USD 160m of IEEPA recovery. No recurring EPS estimate is asserted.

Q3 operating cash flow of USD 2,868m less USD 749m of capital expenditure reconciles to USD 2,119m of free cash flow. Prior-year Q3 free cash flow was USD 4,025m, but nine-month free cash flow increased to USD 6,306m from USD 4,745m. The research therefore does not equate the one-quarter decline with structural cash deterioration.
